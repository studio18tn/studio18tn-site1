Getting Started

Four steps to your first swing

Your application is already on file. Here’s what’s left before you’re on the simulator.

  1. Download the package

    Grab the full package above, or pick individual documents below. Every document comes as a PDF (to view or print) and a Word file (to fill in on your computer).

  2. Sign & return your agreement

    Complete and sign the Membership Agreement & Waiver. Email a signed copy (scan or clear photo is fine) to robertmc@studio18tn.net, or hand it to staff on site.

    We need it back before your first visit.

  3. Set up your TrackMan account

    Download the TrackMan Golf app and create an account using the same email you gave us on your application, then text or email us so we can link it to your membership.

    That account is how you book bay time and track your 15 monthly hours. Read the TrackMan Member Terms below.

  4. Get your access & bring your crew

    Once your signed agreement is in, staff will issue your 24-hour access credential. Keep it to yourself — it’s not shareable.

    Bringing guests? Each guest signs the Guest Waiver on their first visit. Print one ahead of time or sign one on site.
